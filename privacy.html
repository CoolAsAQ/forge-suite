Forge Suite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Forge Suite (including ForgePost) is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channels.

  • It uses Google sign-in only to upload videos and, where enabled, to read view statistics for the owner's own channels.
  • Sign-in tokens and all data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google/YouTube.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Forge Suite's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
