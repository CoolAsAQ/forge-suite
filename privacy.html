Forge Suite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Forge Suite (including ForgePost) is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to schedule and publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It has no other users and no user sign-ups.

What it accesses

  • Google / YouTube: Google sign-in is used to upload videos, read view statistics for the owner's own channels and, where enabled, read and reply to comments on the owner's own videos.
  • TikTok: TikTok sign-in (Login Kit) is used to read the owner's basic profile information and to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account (Content Posting API).
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): Facebook Login is used to publish the owner's own Reels to the owner's own Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages, read their statistics and, where enabled, read and reply to comments on those posts.
  • Public comments: where comment replies are enabled, public comments on the owner's own posts and the commenter's public username are read so the owner can reply. They are stored only on the owner's computer.

Where data goes

  • Sign-in tokens and all data are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Videos are sent only to the platform they are being published to. For Instagram, a video is held for a few minutes in the owner's private Cloudflare R2 storage so Instagram can fetch it, then deleted (anything left is removed automatically within a day).
  • To write post captions and reply drafts, video frames, captions and comment text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Notifications about the owner's own posts are sent to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • No data is sold, shared with any other third party, or used for advertising.

Removing access

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions
  • Meta: Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations)
  • To have any locally stored data deleted, contact the owner at the address below.

Forge Suite's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy, the TikTok Developer Terms of Service, and the Meta Platform Terms.
